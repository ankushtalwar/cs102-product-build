# CS 102 — Rapid Web Development for AI Products

**Tetr · Term 1 India**

This repository is where your team builds its product for the rest of the course. There is no starter code — you and your agent build it here, one session at a time.

> **The rule:** You may use AI to write your code. You must be able to explain any line you ship.

---

## Course objectives

By the end of CS 102 you will be able to:

1. **Find a problem worth solving** — explain why some products stick, and define who your product is for and what it promises them.
2. **Ship to the real internet** — take a page from your laptop to a live URL anyone can open.
3. **Build with AI agents, not just chat with them** — understand what a model actually is, how an agent differs from a chatbot, and why *everything the model knows about your product is whatever is in front of it right now*.
4. **Give your agent the right context** — write and maintain an `AGENTS.md` project instructions file, and steer the agent deliberately instead of hoping for the best.
5. **Design before you build** — sketch and prototype the key screens of your product before generating code.
6. **Plan work as tickets** — break version one into small, clear tickets and take each one from ticket to pull request.
7. **Build a real full-stack product** — add a database, user login, and the tools and skills your agent needs to work well.
8. **Debug with confidence** — find and fix problems in code you did not type yourself.
9. **Decide where AI belongs in your product** — and where it does not.
10. **Build safely** — apply basic security practices and keep agents inside a sandbox.
11. **Work with autonomous and multiple agents** — let agents work alone, then coordinate many agents to ship version two.
12. **Put it in front of real users** — gather feedback, iterate, and present a working product on Demo Day.
13. **Own every line** — explain any component in your codebase. If nobody on the team can explain it, it gets deleted.

## Getting started

1. Click **Use this template** to create your team's own copy of this repository.
2. Add your teammates as collaborators.
3. Replace this README with your product's name, who it is for, and the promise it makes.
4. Add an `AGENTS.md` so your agent knows what you are building.
